\subsection{Exploratory pilot, bootstrap protocol and interaction accounting}
\label{app:experiment_details}

Prior to the main 20-seed study (seeds 1601--1620 on HalfCheetah and Hopper),
an exploratory five-seed 75k benchmark (seeds 1201--1205) evaluated BAMOR and
native CAPQL across HalfCheetah, Hopper and Ant (Table~\ref{tab:final_policy}).
In that initial sweep, BAMOR won four of five direct-utility pairs on both
HalfCheetah and Hopper while losing all five Ant pairs, motivating both the
independent 20-seed HalfCheetah/Hopper evaluation and the dedicated Ant
mechanistic diagnostics in Section~\ref{sec:ant_diagnosis}. Pilot and 20-seed
cohorts remain strictly unpooled.

Both BAMOR-versus-CAPQL protocols prespecified paired percentile bootstrap
intervals with 20,000 whole-seed resamples (NumPy PCG64 seed~0). Each 97.5\%
interval is marginal, with a Bonferroni adjustment for the two environments
within the corresponding protocol. Coverage is approximate. The full-simplex
study was a retrospective baseline completion conducted after the primary
cohort was inspected. It reused 80 existing runs unchanged. Its comparison
of the two CAPQL samplers is secondary and uses descriptive 95\% intervals.
For reference, ordinary 95\% intervals for BAMOR minus native CAPQL are
$[-15.93,220.51]$ on HalfCheetah and $[-48.49,96.88]$ on Hopper. The
corresponding intervals for BAMOR minus full-simplex CAPQL are
$[-84.21,107.67]$ and $[12.77,153.89]$.

In total, the 80 BAMOR and native-CAPQL runs on seeds 1601--1620 consumed
$6{,}000{,}000$ training interactions, $16{,}512{,}980$ evaluation interactions
and $160$ initialization probe steps; the 40 matched full-simplex CAPQL runs
added $3{,}000{,}000$ training interactions, $7{,}692{,}175$ evaluation
interactions and $80$ probes while reusing the saved BAMOR and native-CAPQL
records unchanged (separate prelaunch smoke checks used $2{,}240$ training,
$28{,}224$ evaluation and $56$ probe steps). The five-seed exploratory sweep
consumed $2{,}250{,}000$ training and $6{,}240{,}415$ evaluation interactions.
In the 50k archive experiments (HalfCheetah seeds 1011--1025; Hopper and Ant
seeds 1011--1015), 19 actor snapshots were retained every $2{,}500$ steps from
step $5{,}000$ to $50{,}000$ and evaluated under the final context encoder.
